## Darby's Paper Lab

Most of the site is Darby's Paper Lab, a series of interactive essays on the
papers behind modern sequence modeling. Each essay works through the central
result of a paper and pairs it with a demo that runs the real mechanism in the
browser. The equations in the text are the equations being integrated on screen:
a reader can change a parameter, draw an input, and watch what the model does
with it.

The first entry covers HiPPO, the memory theory underneath S4 and Mamba. Essays
on selective state space models and on the S4 convolution kernel are in progress.
